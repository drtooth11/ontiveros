What are cookies?

A cookie is a file that is downloaded to your computer when you visit certain web pages. Among other things, cookies allow a website to store and retrieve information about the browsing habits of a user or their device and, depending on the information they contain and how you use your device, they can be used to recognise the user. Third-party cookies:

These are cookies installed on a website that do not belong to the website being visited. They are therefore specific to a third party's domain and the information they contain can be viewed and managed by that third party. These cookies may also be used to show you advertisements tailored to your interests. If the user clicks on a banner, they will be taken to the advertiser's URL, where cookies will be used to refine information about their preferences.
